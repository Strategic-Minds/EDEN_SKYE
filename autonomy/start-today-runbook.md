### 5. Launch The First 72-Hour Sprint

Post and measure aggressively, but do not use fake engagement.

Minimum cadence:

- TikTok: 4 posts per day
- Instagram: 2 Reels and 3 Stories per day
- Facebook: 2 brand posts per day
- Personal page: 1 founder bridge post per day

Every post gets tagged:

- Brand: Eden / Epoxy / AI Can't Do This / Personal Bridge
- Emotional engine: Fear / Hope / Desire / Proof / Identity
- Funnel stage: Attention / Trust / Lead / Sale
- CTA: Follow / Comment / Free Tool / Starter Pack / Shopify

### 6. Paid Spend Rules

Do not boost brand-new untested content.

Spend only after organic test signals:

- strong 3-second hold
- strong completion or replay rate
- above-average shares/saves/comments
- profile visits
- follow conversion
- Shopify clicks or lead captures

Initial paid plan:

- 20 percent retargeting traffic to free tool or starter pack
- 40 percent amplify winning short videos
- 30 percent creator/UGC seeding
- 10 percent production and editing support

### 7. Daily Review

Each day, classify every post:

- Kill
- Improve
- Clone

Clone winners into:

- a new hook
- a new opening visual
- a new caption
- a personal-page version
- a Shopify CTA version
- an Eden reaction version
- an AI Can't Do This contrast version

## Automation Method

Cron should trigger checks, not make unchecked decisions.

Recommended automation chain:

1. Cron checks queue and provider readiness
2. Auto Builder pulls approved drafts
3. Supabase logs queue claim, action, evidence, blocker, and result
4. Metricool/Xyla receive only draft-safe or approved content
5. Analytics return to scoring sheet
6. Winner-cloning packet is generated
7. Any public publish or paid action waits for approval

## Approval Matrix

Human approval required for:

- public posting
- paid ad launch
- Shopify change
- Supabase schema/data mutation
- Vercel production deploy
- external email/SMS send
- payment or discount change
- use of customer faces, testimonials, or income claims
- product specs, warranty, training, or certification claims
